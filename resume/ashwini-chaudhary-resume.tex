\cvevent{Chief Technology Officer}{Diversio Inc}{Jul 2024 -- Present}{Toronto}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{New product, new market.} Led Diversio's pivot to Optimo, from internal launch (Nov 2025) to paying customers and pilots. I own the roadmap with customer success and sales, and still ship features myself.
\item \textbf{AI across the company.} On my own initiative, built Courage, an AI teammate in Slack and GitHub used by sales, customer success, and consulting as well as engineering. In its first month it handled about 3,800 requests, reviewed 840+ PRs, and opened 87, and it now runs most of our old manual processes. People approve every change that matters.
\item \textbf{Review stopped being the bottleneck.} Getting good human reviews used to slow every release. Our own AI reviewer has done 4,700+ reviews across 1,150+ PRs against our standards, so humans review what actually needs judgment, with AI spend kept low.
\item \textbf{AI in the product, with privacy built in.} Set Optimo's AI direction and shipped Lumi, an AI assistant for managers, designed so no individual's data reaches AI providers. Moved clients' sensitive HR files off email into a secure exchange in the product.
\item \textbf{Trust as a selling point.} Ran our first SOC 2 Type 2 end to end as the main contact with the auditor: wrote the security policies, did the vendor reviews and risk register, and automated most evidence capture with AI agents. Launched a public Trust Center with a staff guide, so sales and customer success can answer security requests without engineering.
\item \textbf{Lean by default.} Cut our AWS bill by about two-thirds in a year, brought CI costs to almost zero since February by moving checks onto our own local CI, and cut deploys from 8--13 minutes to about 2.5. A small team ships around three backend releases a week.
\item \textbf{Beyond the job description.} As the team got smaller, took on compliance, IT, vendor negotiations, hiring and onboarding, and hands-on help for sales and customer success. When our cloud dev-environment vendor shut down, moved those environments onto spare in-house hardware at no added cost.
\item \textbf{Teaching the company.} Run weekly sessions, write guides, and gave a company town hall talk on working with AI agents, so the whole team, not just engineers, gets value from them. I also write publicly about how we work.
\item \textbf{Team.} Hire and mentor engineers and co-ops (UofT, Queen's, Waterloo) through skill-based live interviews, and built the developer tooling the team runs on, including open-source local CI.
\end{itemize}
