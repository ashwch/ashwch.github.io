\cvevent{Chief Technology Officer}{Diversio Inc}{Jul 2024 -- Present}{Toronto}
\begin{itemize}
\item Lead Diversio's pivot to Optimo and own engineering end to end, from architecture and technical strategy to shipping features in both products myself.
\item Built \textbf{little-person}, our automated PR reviewer: a Temporal-backed worker that reviews against our own standards and only spends a deep review on PRs that are ready, which keeps token spend low. It has reviewed 1,100+ PRs and took human review out of the critical path for the team.
\item Built \textbf{Courage}, the engineering agent the whole company now uses in Slack and GitHub, on Pi Durable. It investigates issues, opens PRs, does a lighter automatic review on every PR alongside little-person, triages Sentry and infra alerts, runs sandboxes, and posts deploy and cost feeds, routing each request to a quick, standard, or deep model tier, behind host guards that block merges and secret leaks.
\item Built \textbf{Dev Center} (Rust), which gives every worktree its own services, database, and URLs and hands its state to Claude, Codex, or Pi, and \textbf{local-ci-runner} (Go, open source), which runs checks locally and posts GitHub statuses only for verified snapshots.
\item Wrote Pi extensions (pi-monitor, ci-status, image-router, pi-cmux) and 23 Claude Code/Codex plugins in a public marketplace the whole team uses.
\item Cut the AWS bill by about a third over the past year while keeping production in SOC 2 scope, and moved CI, preview environments, and internal tools in-house where that was cheaper and fit us better.
\item Part of Diversio's first SOC 2; now run this year's audit largely on my own across engineering, infrastructure, and admin, with most checks and evidence automated.
\item Migrated most of the company's older manual processes onto Courage and cloud agents, and moved every credential and API key off people's laptops into 1Password and managed secrets.
\item Led vendor rationalization and a successful SR\&ED round; migrated our API workflow from Postman to Bruno (the post was later featured in Bruno's marketing).
\item Hired and mentored engineers and co-op students from the University of Toronto, Queen's, Waterloo, and other top programs.
\end{itemize}
